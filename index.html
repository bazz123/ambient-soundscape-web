Form

Arcs shape density, brightness and layer levels over the length. Sunrise holds its end state; Bloom and Sleep end in silence and stop. The same seed with the same settings plays the same soundscape, and the seed is kept in the page link. Arc and seed changes apply on the next START.

Layers

The freeze pad holds the spectrum at the scan head as an endless, seamless tone and refreezes on every scene change.

The freeze pad needs AudioWorklet support, which this view doesn't allow. The other layers still work.

Grain texture

Scan speed moves a playhead through the essence: 0 holds one spot, 1× is the original speed, negative runs backwards. Spray scatters grains around it.

Export

Renders faster than real time with the same seed and settings as live playback. Length and arc come from Form, the loudness target from the meter. Playback stops while it renders.

Character & tape

Character
Tape

Drift sets how often scenes change, from every 10 s to every 34 s. Brightness opens or closes the overall tone. The tape stage adds wow and flutter, saturation and hiss to everything.

Space

Binaural mode places grains and shimmer around your head with HRTF panning. The grain cloud slowly orbits you, and farther sounds get duller and quieter. Drones and pads stay a stereo bed.
