Scan speed moves a playhead through the essence: 0 holds one spot, 1× is the original speed, negative runs backwards. Spray scatters grains around it. The freeze pad holds the spectrum at the scan head as an endless, seamless tone and refreezes on every scene change. Drift sets how often scenes change.

The freeze pad needs AudioWorklet support, which this view doesn't allow. The other layers still work.
